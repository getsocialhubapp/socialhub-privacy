SocialHub — Privacy Policy

Effective: September 24, 2026

The short version

SocialHub collects no personal data. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no server receiving your information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. SocialHub does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or to any third party on our behalf.

On-device data

To keep you signed in, each social network's website stores its own cookies and site data on your device, exactly as a web browser would. This data never leaves your device except when communicating directly with the network you are visiting. You can delete all of it at any time via Settings → Clear Browsing Data.

Face ID / device passcode

If you enable App Lock, authentication is handled entirely by iOS on your device. We never see, store, or transmit your biometric data.

Tracker Blocking & Focus Mode

These Pro features run entirely on-device. No browsing information is sent anywhere as part of blocking trackers or hiding short-video feeds.

Purchases

SocialHub Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system and processed by Apple. We do not see or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs the purchase itself.

Third-party networks

SocialHub loads the public mobile websites of X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads, LinkedIn, Reddit, YouTube, and Snapchat. Each network operates under its own privacy policy and may collect data according to its terms once you sign in. SocialHub is not affiliated with, endorsed, or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc., X Corp., TikTok Inc., Google LLC, Microsoft Corporation (LinkedIn), Reddit, Inc., or Snap Inc.

Children's privacy

SocialHub is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
